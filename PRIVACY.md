# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 28, 2026

## 1. Introduction

UPSC-BPSC is an educational platform designed to help students prepare for UPSC and BPSC examinations through current affairs, MCQs, answer writing, and AI-powered learning features.

We respect user privacy and are committed to protecting personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the platform may collect:

* Name
* Email address
* Account authentication information
* Exam preferences such as UPSC or BPSC
* Learning activity and submitted answers
* Questions and answers generated or submitted through the platform
* Payment-related transaction information where applicable

## 3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

* Create and manage user accounts
* Provide educational services
* Personalize learning features
* Generate and evaluate answer-writing content
* Process subscriptions and payments
* Improve platform performance and reliability
* Prevent unauthorized access and misuse
* Provide customer support

## 4. AI Services

Some educational features may use third-party AI services to generate questions, model answers, explanations, or evaluations.

Only information necessary to provide the requested AI feature should be sent to such services.

## 5. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

The application does not intentionally store sensitive payment credentials such as card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, or banking passwords.

## 6. API Keys and Secrets

API keys, database credentials, JWT secrets, payment secrets, and other confidential configuration values must be stored as environment variables.

Users and contributors should **never commit `.env` files or private API keys to the Git repository**.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect user information, including:

* Password hashing
* Authentication and authorization
* Environment-based secret management
* Restricted access to confidential configuration
* Secure communication where HTTPS is available

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention

User information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, comply with applicable requirements, or resolve disputes.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The platform may use third-party services for functions such as:

* AI processing
* Payment processing
* Database hosting
* Application hosting
* News or content APIs

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The platform is intended primarily for educational examination preparation. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when platform functionality, technology, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published in the repository.

## 12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the platform administrator through the contact information provided by the application.
